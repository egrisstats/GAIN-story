The decision tree

How the Secretariat reviews an example where the respondent says the recommendations were not used, or does not know. We answer the questions ourselves from what the example shows, score the evidence, and edit when the score is high enough, then tell the respondent what we changed. A Don't know and a No go through the same steps; a No needs a higher score and a sign that the recommendations were used.

The respondent's answer
Thresholds: a Don't know is edited at 4 points; a No at 7 points including a sign of use. Below 7, a No with 4 or more points is recorded as aligned. Paragraphs from IRRS (2018), IRIS (2020) and IROSS (2023). EGRISS Secretariat.
